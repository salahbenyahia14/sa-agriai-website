## Bibliographic record required for every published article

Every article, field sheet, alert or animal-care page must store:

- Unique source ID.
- Exact document title.
- Author, institution or responsible department.
- Direct URL or stable document identifier.
- Publication date or edition; mark it unknown when not present.
- Date accessed by the editorial team.
- Page number(s), section(s) or table(s) supporting each material claim.
- Article language and source language.
- Crop, species, production system and geographic scope.
- Validity/review date, especially for alerts and seasonal recommendations.
- Reviewer name/role and review status.
- A note distinguishing direct source content from SA AgriAI explanation or interpretation.

## Publication rules

1. No article is published without at least one traceable source. High-impact or safety-sensitive recommendations should be corroborated by a second authoritative source or reviewed by the appropriate specialist.
2. Never invent publication dates, page numbers, authors, numerical values, citations or institutional endorsements.
3. Separate confirmed source statements from editorial explanation and from unknown/missing information.
4. Do not publish medication doses, veterinary prescriptions, pesticide recipes or chemical mixing instructions based only on a general article or a search snippet. Refer users to a veterinarian or authorised plant-protection specialist.
5. Do not reuse old seasonal bulletins as current alerts. Store the issue date, location, crop/species and validity period.
6. Localise recommendations by wilaya/agroecological zone where the source supports it. Never assume one region's timing or rate applies to all Algeria.
7. Link to the original source. Do not upload or republish third-party PDFs/images without checking reuse rights.
8. Re-review articles when official guidance, product registrations, disease status or production conditions change.

## Review status vocabulary

- Verified source: the originating official/institutional record and direct link were checked.
- Bibliography complete: author/institution, title, date/edition and locator/page details recorded.
- Specialist reviewed: an appropriately qualified reviewer checked the interpretation.
- Published: all required fields and review approvals are complete.
- Needs review: incomplete, outdated or conflicting material; do not present as current advice.

**Important:** Being listed in this register does not mean every document has already been fully reviewed for publication. Each source document must be checked individually.
